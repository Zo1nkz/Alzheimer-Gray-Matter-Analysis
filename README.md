# Alzheimer-Gray-Matter-Analysis

Does gray matter volume in the frontal and left parietal lobes help distinguish
cognitively normal adults from those with cognitive impairment? This project
explores that question with a 2,700-participant subset of the National
Alzheimer's Coordinating Center (NACC) dataset, controlling for years of education.

Completed for MATH 220 (Statistics), Spring 2026.

<ins>For the most essential explanation and details of the study, viewing the full report is highly recommended.</ins>

__Key Findings:__ Based on the the t-test and chi-square results, there were significant correlations between the diagnosis of the patients with Alzheimer's Disease and the demographics they belonged to such as education level (high school diploma, bachelor's, etc.).

__Methods:__ Data wrangling, EDA, data visualization, and domain knowledge.

__Repository Navigation:__ R Markdown analyses in `analysis/`, the NACC dataset in `data/`, and the final written report in `report/`.

__How to Run:__ open the .Rproj, install packages, and knit 01 → 03.

__Data Source:__ For more information data collection, API's, and Alzheimer's Disease in general, visit: https://www.naccdata.org/
